The movement

Every ward. Every street. Every door.

The PBAT Door-to-Door Movement is a nationwide grassroots campaign taking President Tinubu's Renewed Hope agenda to Nigerians where they live, one home at a time, to secure his re-election in 2027.

Founder & Convener

High Chief Government Oweizide Ekpemupolo (Tompolo)

Tompolo is a Niger Delta leader who founded, funds and leads the PBAT Door-to-Door Movement. He heads Tantita Security Services Nigeria Limited, engaged by the NNPC in 2022 to protect oil pipelines across the Niger Delta, work widely credited with helping to curb oil theft and restore crude output.

Through the movement he is putting coordinators, trained ambassadors and campaign vehicles in every state, so that every Nigerian hears directly about the President's reforms and is ready to vote in 2027.

“Become ambassadors and translate the registered voters to votes.”Message from Tompolo to the movement's Ondo coordinators, October 2026
