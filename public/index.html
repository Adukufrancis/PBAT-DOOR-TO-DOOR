About the President

From Lagos to Aso Rock

Bola Ahmed Tinubu served in the Senate, led Lagos State for two terms, and was sworn in as President at Eagle Square, Abuja, on 29 May 2023.

  1. 29 MARCH 1952

    Born. This is the generally accepted date of birth.

  2. 1992 – 1993

    Senator representing Lagos West in the Third Republic, until the Senate was dissolved in 1993.

  3. 1999 – 2007

    Governor of Lagos State for two terms.

  4. 29 MAY 2023

    Sworn in as the 16th President of Nigeria. On the same day he announced the end of the petrol subsidy.

  5. 24 OCTOBER 2026

    Re-election campaign launched in Kaduna.
