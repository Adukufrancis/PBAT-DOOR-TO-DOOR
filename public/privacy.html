Privacy

Privacy policy

Last updated

Draft for review by the movement's legal adviser before launch.

This policy explains what personal data ("we") collects through this website, why, and the choices you have. We process your data in line with the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023.

Who is responsible for your data

The data controller is , . For anything about your data, email .

What we collect

  • Sign-in details: your email address or phone number, and the one-time codes used to confirm them.
  • Profile details you choose to give: full name, gender, date of birth, state, LGA, ward, polling unit, address, occupation and how you'd like to help. Only name, state and LGA are required.
  • Your choices: when you agreed to join and to this policy, and whether you asked for SMS/WhatsApp updates.
  • Technical data: which devices are logged in to your account (browser and device type), and your IP address for security and to stop abuse.
  • Location (only if you tap "Find polling units near me"): your phone's position is used once to suggest nearby polling units. We do not store it.

Because this is a political movement, the fact that you signed up may reveal your political opinion. We treat all of your data as sensitive.

Why we use it

  • To create and secure your account and keep you logged in.
  • To connect you with volunteers and coordinators in your state, LGA, ward and polling unit, and to organise outreach.
  • To send you updates by SMS or WhatsApp, only if you ticked that box.
  • To keep the site safe (limiting code requests, blocking abuse).

We rely on your consent, which you give by ticking the box when you sign up. You can withdraw it at any time (see "Your rights").

Who we share it with

We do not sell your data. We share it only with service providers that run the site for us, under their data-protection terms:

  • Render (website hosting) and Neon (database), which store data on our behalf.
  • Google (email delivery of sign-in codes, and Firebase for phone sign-in codes).
  • Any customer-relationship tool we use to manage volunteers (for example Zoho CRM). We will update this list before adding a new provider.

Movement coordinators may see the details of volunteers in their own area so they can organise outreach. Some providers may store data outside Nigeria, with appropriate safeguards.

How long we keep it

We keep your account while it is active and for up to 12 months after the 2027 general elections, unless you ask us to delete it sooner. One-time codes are deleted as soon as they are used or expire (10 minutes).

Your rights

You can ask us to: see the data we hold about you; correct it; delete your account and data; give you a copy; or stop SMS/WhatsApp updates. You can also withdraw your consent at any time. Email from the email or phone number you signed up with, and we will reply within 30 days.

You can update most details yourself on your account page. If you are not satisfied with our answer, you can complain to the Nigeria Data Protection Commission (NDPC).

Security

We use encrypted connections (HTTPS), store only a scrambled copy of sign-in codes and session tokens, limit how often codes can be requested, and restrict who can see volunteer lists.

Children

This site is for people aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top and, for important changes, tell you when you next log in.
