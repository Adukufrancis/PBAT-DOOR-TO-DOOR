Five things to tell every household

  1. Students can now study on a loan. Through NELFUND, ₦242.4 billion has reached 1,388,592 students in 288 institutions (to April 2026).
  2. Workers earn more. The national minimum wage went from ₦30,000 to ₦70,000 in July 2024, and is now reviewed every three years.
  3. Fairer taxes. Four new tax laws, in force from January 2026, give relief to low-income earners, families and small businesses.
  4. Roads that link the country. The Lagos–Calabar Coastal Highway (750 km, 9 states) and 81 km of the Abuja–Kaduna–Zaria–Kano road are open.
  5. The economy is growing. GDP grew 4.43% in Q2 2026, and ₦5.41 trillion is going into security in the 2026 budget.

Sources for each figure are on the His works section when you're back online.
