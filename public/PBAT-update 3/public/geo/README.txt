Polling-unit lists used by the sign-up form (state > LGA > ward > polling unit, with GPS where INEC has it).
176,846 polling units, 8,810 wards, 774 LGAs across 36 states and the FCT.
Source: INEC's Continuous Voter Registration portal, as compiled in
https://github.com/kcemenike/find-my-polling-unit (inec_scraped/). Rebuilt into one compact file per state.
Each polling unit is [name, code, lat, lng]; lat/lng are missing where INEC has no location.
