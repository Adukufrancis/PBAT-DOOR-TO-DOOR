Terms of use

Last updated

Draft for review by the movement's legal adviser before launch.

These terms apply when you use this website or join as a volunteer. By signing up you agree to them and to our Privacy policy.

Who can join

You must be 18 or older and give true information about yourself. One account per person. Keep access to your email or phone secure, because that is how you log in.

How volunteers should act

  • Be respectful and peaceful at every door. Never use threats, insults, bribes or pressure.
  • Share only accurate information. Use the sourced facts on this site, and don't make up figures or promises.
  • Respect people's privacy: only collect someone's details with their clear permission, and never share them outside the movement.
  • Follow the law, including the Electoral Act and INEC's rules and timetable.
  • Don't present yourself as an official of the government, INEC or any security agency.

We may suspend or remove accounts that break these rules.

Content on this site

We work to keep facts accurate and link each one to its source, but figures can change. If you spot an error, please tell us. You may share our content to support the movement; please don't change it in a misleading way. The logo and name belong to the movement.

Official accounts

Our only official social media accounts are listed on the Contact page. Be careful of fake pages asking for money or personal details.

Availability and liability

We provide the site as it is and may change or pause it at any time. As far as the law allows, we are not responsible for losses caused by using the site or by links to other websites.
